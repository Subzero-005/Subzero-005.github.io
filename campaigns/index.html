Campaign Performance Evaluator

24 ad campaigns across 6 channels over 90 days. Each campaign gets a 0–100 score and an A–F grade with a recommendation; unusual days are flagged with a likely cause; the budget is reallocated at the same total spend; and a random-forest model forecasts conversions at different spend levels.
